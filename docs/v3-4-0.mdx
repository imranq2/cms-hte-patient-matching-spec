## What changed

The specification's Executive Summary lists these changes. Each links to the proposal ([GitHub issue](https://github.com/imranq2/cms-hte-patient-matching-spec/issues)) that produced it.

### Added or changed

- **Table 2 is split into two categories** ([Proposal v3.3.1](https://github.com/imranq2/cms-hte-patient-matching-spec/issues/1)).

  - Category 1: 30 rules that already carry an individuating field, evaluated in one step, unchanged from v3.3.0.
  - Category 2: 8 rules (7 amended, 1 new), evaluated in two disjoint steps: a Household Match, then an Individual Match within that household.
  - See [Table 2: Category 1 Rules](/docs/table-2-category-1-rules) and [Category 2: Household and Individual Rules](/docs/category-2-household-and-individual-rules).
- **Two new rules, 39 and 40**, for guardian-verified minors and for newborn matching via maternal linkage ([Proposal v3.3.3](https://github.com/imranq2/cms-hte-patient-matching-spec/issues/3)). See [Category 2: Household and Individual Rules](/docs/category-2-household-and-individual-rules) and [Applying the Tables](/docs/applying-the-tables).
- **Placeholder-value handling is extended** from names to every field Table 2 relies on ([Proposal v3.3.4](https://github.com/imranq2/cms-hte-patient-matching-spec/issues/4)). See [Placeholder Values](/docs/placeholder-values).
- <strong>Compliance is split into Path A (Assumed Compliance, rules-based) and Path B (Empirical Compliance, performance-based)</strong> ([Proposal v3.3.2](https://github.com/imranq2/cms-hte-patient-matching-spec/issues/2)). See [Audit and Monitoring](/docs/audit-and-monitoring).
- **A new commitment to an empirically validated accuracy floor** ([Proposal v3.3.5](https://github.com/imranq2/cms-hte-patient-matching-spec/issues/5)). See [Core Principles](/docs/core-principles) (Principle 13) and [Validation Requirements](/docs/validation-requirements).

### Removed

None listed in the Executive Summary.
